About SparkFest

Where Passion Meets Performance

SparkFest is the premier inter-collegiate cultural festival, bringing together the brightest minds, the most talented performers, and the most creative artists under one virtual roof. Hosted annually, it offers a platform to exhibit skills in music, dance, digital arts, coding, theater, and gaming.

Quick System Guidelines

Welcome to the SparkFest Management portal. As an Administrator, you can:

1. Manage Events: Add new items, update schedules/venues, or delete events instantly.
2. Approve Registrations: View submissions, search by name, and trigger ticket approval/rejections. Approved tickets dynamically grant green passes.
3. Announce Updates: Broadcast real-time tickers that reflect immediately on the participant bulletin.
4. Declare Winners: Select any completed event and register the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place holders.
